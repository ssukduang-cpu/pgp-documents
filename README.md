# pgp-documents

These are copies of **public-record documents** from the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office (PTAB decisions, including designated precedential and informative decisions, USPTO guidance, and MPEP chapters) and from the federal courts (Federal Circuit and Supreme Court opinions). They support postgrantproceeding.com.

- **Source and retrieval.** Every file was downloaded directly from the official public URL listed in `metadata.json` / `metadata.csv` (`source_url`) on the date shown (`retrieved_date`). Its SHA-256 matched an earlier independent download. Each listing should read: *"Copy from USPTO (or the named court), retrieved <date>."* The official source controls if a copy differs or is later withdrawn.
- **Status and designation.** `designation` and `status` reflect the USPTO precedential/informative list as of the retrieval date. Designations change, so check the [USPTO page](https://www.uspto.gov/patents/ptab/precedential-informative-decisions) for current status.
- **Scope.** Only government-issued decisions, orders, and guidance are included. Sealed or protective-order papers, party filings, and third-party publications are not.
- **Not legal advice.** These copies are provided for convenience and research. Nothing here is legal advice, and no attorney-client relationship is created.
- **No affiliation.** This collection is unaffiliated with Polsinelli PC or any client. It contains no client materials and no firm work product.

Files are distributed as GitHub Release assets. See the Releases page and `metadata.json`.
